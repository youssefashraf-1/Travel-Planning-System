# Bonus features

Optional workbook items, implemented on 20 November 2026 after every mandatory requirement was complete. Each Swagger summary ends with "(optional)".

| Requirement                         | Endpoints                                                                               | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ACC-02:** email verification      | `GET /api/auth/verify-email?token=`, `POST /api/auth/resend-verification`               | Self-registered travelers get an emailed link (valid 24 h, single use; a new link cancels older ones). Until they verify, `emailVerified` is `false` and holding a flight or reserving a hotel returns 403. Admins, moderators and accounts created before this feature count as verified.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **ACC-07:** forgot password         | `POST /api/auth/forgot-password`, `POST /api/auth/reset-password`                       | The reply is always the same 202, so it never reveals whether an email has an account. The link goes to `FRONTEND_URL/reset-password?token=…`; it lasts 30 minutes, works once, and a newer request cancels it. A reset ends all sessions and clears any lockout.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **HTL-26:** review replies          | `POST /api/reviews/:id/reply`                                                           | Only the moderator of the review's hotel can reply. Posting again replaces the reply, which is shown under the review in `GET /api/hotels/:id/reviews`. Hidden reviews cannot be answered.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **BLG-13:** itinerary on a post     | `PATCH /api/posts/:id/itinerary`                                                        | The author attaches one of their own public itineraries, or sends `null` to remove it. Readers see it on the post only while it stays public.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **ITN-27:** bookings in itineraries | `POST /api/itineraries/:id/bookings`, `DELETE /api/itineraries/:id/bookings/:linkId`    | Links the owner's confirmed or completed bookings to a day: by default the day of the flight's departure or the hotel's check-in. Flights count as intercity transport and hotels as accommodation in the budget, while the booking stays confirmed or completed. Booking charges stay in EGP (PAY-04); the budget only converts them for planning. Clones start without linked bookings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **ITN-28:** itinerary PDF           | `GET /api/itineraries/:id/pdf?currency=`                                                | Owner only. Includes the route, days, activities, budget entries, transport and budget totals.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **BON-01:** admin analytics         | `GET /api/admin/analytics?fromDate=&toDate=&providerType=`                              | Bookings paid in the period (default the last 30 days, at most 366), per airline and hotel: bookings, later cancellations, gross revenue, refunds and net revenue in EGP. A round trip on two airlines is split by each segment's fares and bag fees.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **FLT-27:** change a flight         | `PATCH /api/flight-bookings/:id/change-flight`                                          | Moves one flight of a confirmed booking to another date or flight on the same route and cabin, before it departs. `previewOnly: true` shows the fare difference first. Passenger types are rechecked on the new date and extra bags are repriced with the new airline's policy. A same or lower total changes at once and refunds the difference to the card through Stripe. A higher total holds the new seats for 10 minutes while the traveler pays the difference with `POST /api/payments` and `bookingType: "flight_change"`; unpaid holds expire. A later cancellation refund is split over the original and fare-difference payments. |
| **FLT-28:** seat selection          | `GET /api/flights/:id/seats?cabinClass=`, `PATCH /api/flight-bookings/:id/seat`         | A public seat map per cabin (first and business rows of 4 from rows 1 and 3, premium economy and economy rows of 6 from rows 12 and 20). Travelers pick a seat per passenger and flight on a held or confirmed booking; infants have no seat. A unique index means two people can never get the same seat, even at the same moment. Seats are freed when a booking is cancelled, expires or changes flight, and appear on the e-ticket.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **BON-02:** favourites              | `POST /api/favourites`, `GET /api/favourites?type=`, `DELETE /api/favourites/:type/:id` | Travelers save active hotels and other people's public itineraries. Items that later stop being available stay listed with `available: false`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

**Email delivery (GEN-01-B groundwork):**

- `src/common/mail/mail.service.ts` sends through SMTP using `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS` and `MAIL_FROM`. Without `SMTP_HOST`, it prints each email (with its link) in the server console for local demos.
- Links are never returned by the API.
- Tokens are random and stored only as SHA-256 digests (`accounttokens`), and MongoDB deletes them a day after they expire.

Not implemented: the rest of GEN-01-B (emailing every in-app notification).

**Tests:** `test/bonus-features.e2e-spec.ts` and `test/flight-changes-seats.e2e-spec.ts` cover each feature, including permissions and error cases. Emails are captured by a fake mail service.
